Road Accident Fund Claims

If the worst happens, we handle the rest.

The RAF supports anyone injured or killed in a road accident caused by third-party negligence. Claims are technical, slow, and expensive without help — your membership covers all of it, end to end.

What you get

  • 100% payout from the RAF

    You keep the full settlement at the end of your claim.

  • Complimentary administration & claims management

    We run the process from intake to settlement.

  • No-cost legal representation

    Qualified attorneys at no charge to you.

  • Free medical, legal & actuarial reports

    Every report needed to prove and value your claim.

  • Loss of support & loss of earnings reports

    Detailed actuarial work to maximise your settlement.

Need to claim?

Speak to our claims team during office hours. Have your ID number or All Road membership number ready and we'll guide you from there.

FAQ & Terms

The small print, explained.

PROJECTHELP (Pty) Ltd, as the Administrator of your membership, arranges for a payment instruction to be sent to Wesbank to add R89 (Single) or R159 (Family) to your existing Volkswagen Financial Services monthly debit order. Should Wesbank be unable to approve the addition due to creditworthiness considerations, we'll debit the existing bank account on file with Wesbank. Payments appear with the reference "ProjectHelp".
No waiting period for licence renewal. You'll receive monthly in-app fines reports and can claim licence renewal benefits immediately. Fines Assist activates once your first debit order has been successfully collected.
Cancel your membership any time with 30 days' notice by emailing allroad@projecthelp.co.za. Subscription fees are payable monthly in advance. No refunds will be allowed on monthly payments.
You get unlimited call-outs every 12 months. If the service is abused (used for events other than emergencies), you may be asked to pay R450 (excl. VAT) per additional call-out. Genuine emergency activations are always covered.
Project Help covers dispatch of medical and ambulance services to you in an emergency. Stabilisation is included. If hospital transportation is needed, the bill is for your account or your medical aid (the emergency provider may bill your medical aid directly). Telephonic medical advice, trauma support, and emergency information are all included.

For full terms, visit projecthelp.co.za/terms.
No — RAF Assist is not an insurance product. It's a service to help you claim from the South African Road Accident Fund. The accident must have happened within South Africa. Claims must be lodged within 3 years (2 years for hit-and-run). We don't guarantee a pay-out from the RAF, but we'll make every reasonable effort to reach a settlement, and we cover all administration, legal, medical and actuarial costs.
Licence renewal is available across all 9 provinces: Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, Western Cape, Limpopo, Free State, Northern Cape, Mpumalanga, and North West. The covered municipal areas may be expanded or contracted from time to time without prior notice.
